Retail Sales Analytics Project

1. Project Overview

The Retail Sales Analytics project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to evaluate business performance, identify profitable products, understand customer purchasing behavior, and discover opportunities to improve sales and profitability.

This project uses data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL queries, Excel analysis, and Power BI visualizations to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

2. Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.

Identify top-performing and low-performing products.

Evaluate sales and profitability across regions.

Analyze customer segments and purchasing patterns.

Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.

Develop interactive dashboards for business reporting.

Provide data-driven recommendations for business improvement.
